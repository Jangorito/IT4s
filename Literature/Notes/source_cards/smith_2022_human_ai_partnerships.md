## Summary

Smith discusses human-AI partnerships in generative music, focusing on creative autonomy, performer control, and how AI systems affect musical behaviour. The paper is useful for IT4s because it frames AI music systems as interaction partners whose value depends on how they participate in the creative process, not only on the output they generate. It is especially helpful for positioning IT4s as an interpretable collaborator within a structured interaction loop.
