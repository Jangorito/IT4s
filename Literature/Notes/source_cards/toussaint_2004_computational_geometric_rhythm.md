## Summary

Toussaint describes musical rhythm using cyclic binary strings, where onsets and silences are represented over a repeated temporal cycle. The paper links rhythmic properties such as evenness and spacing to computational and mathematical analysis. This is useful for IT4s because `PatternTurn` similarly represents rhythm over a quantised grid using active and inactive steps, even though IT4s additionally stores velocity and timing context.
