## Summary

Gillick et al. explore models that translate abstract musical ideas into expressive drum performances using inverse sequence transformations. The work introduces the Groove MIDI Dataset and demonstrates methods for humanization and drum-performance generation. For IT4s, this source is most useful as a future-work comparison: it shows how learned models can add expressive timing and performance detail, while IT4s currently prioritises interpretable capture, analysis, planning, and interim transformation.
