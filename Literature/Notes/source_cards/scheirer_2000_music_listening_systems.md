## Summary

Scheirer examines how humans rapidly orient themselves in music and develops computational models for early stages of music listening. The dissertation covers tempo perception, auditory scene segmentation, and feature extraction from complex musical sounds. For IT4s, this is relevant as broad machine-listening background, especially for justifying the need to extract interpretable features from live musical input before response planning.
