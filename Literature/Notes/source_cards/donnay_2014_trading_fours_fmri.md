## Summary

Donnay et al. study interactive jazz improvisation using trading fours as the experimental model. The paper frames interactive generative musical performance as a form of communication involving unpredictable, collaborative, and emergent exchange. The findings link interactive improvisation to brain areas associated with syntactic processing, supporting the idea that musical exchange has a dialogic structure. For IT4s, this is the key source for motivating trading fours as more than a stylistic reference: it is a structured model of musical interaction.
