## Summary

Petit and Serrano present a generative music approach based on synchronous reactive programming. Their system combines musical patterns with stochastic events and reactive execution, allowing complex musical structures to be produced in real time. This is relevant to IT4s because it supports the idea that real-time music systems benefit from explicit state, events, and controlled nondeterminism. It also provides a useful comparison for IT4s' event-driven orchestration.
