## Summary

Toussaint explores algorithmic methods for generating rhythmic timelines with musically desirable structural properties, including interlocking reflection rhythms and toggle rhythms. The work is not an interactive system paper, but it is valuable background for rhythm generation and for thinking about complementarity, onset placement, and cyclic structure. It can support discussion of future response realisation in IT4s, especially if generated responses are later constrained by rhythmic spacing and complementarity.
