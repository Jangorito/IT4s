## Summary

Levitin, Grahn, and London review research on rhythm, meter, movement, synchronization, entrainment, groove, and other temporal aspects of music cognition. The paper is directly useful for IT4s because the project treats rhythm as the primary medium of human-AI interaction. It supports the argument that timing, pulse, and rhythmic structure are central to how music is perceived and coordinated.
