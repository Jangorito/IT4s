## Summary

Eigenfeldt reviews the first year of the musebot project, in which autonomous software agents create music collaboratively with other agents. The paper is useful because it provides a clear example of musical metacreation where creative agency is primarily within the software ensemble. IT4s differs by situating the human performer inside the interaction loop and using turn-taking as the main collaboration structure.
