## Summary

Hoffman and Weinberg present Shimon, a robotic marimba player that listens to and improvises with a human performer. The system combines musical response with physical gesture and anticipatory action. The paper includes a study manipulating embodiment and response accuracy in a call-and-response task, making it especially useful for evaluation discussions. For IT4s, Shimon is a strong comparison point for real-time interactive musical partnership, even though IT4s is software/hardware-based rather than robotic.
