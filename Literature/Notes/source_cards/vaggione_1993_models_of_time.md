## Summary

Vaggione critiques simplistic oppositions between determinism and randomness in computer-aided composition. The paper argues that music cannot be reduced to a one-dimensional continuum between fixed determinism and stochastic behaviour, because musical form operates across multiple interacting temporal levels. For IT4s, this supports the design choice of using deterministic turn orchestration and explicit constraints while leaving space for variation in response planning and future generation.
