## Summary

Hu et al. address call-and-response as an automatic composition problem, introducing a dataset of call-response pairs and a knowledge-enhanced learning-based model. The paper emphasises rhythm, melody, harmony, and objective evaluation metrics. For IT4s, it is useful as a contrast: IT4s is live, turn-based, rhythmic, and interpretable, whereas this work is dataset/model-centred and broader in musical domain.
