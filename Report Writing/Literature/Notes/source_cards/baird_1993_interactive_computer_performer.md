## Summary

Baird, Blevins, and Zahler describe an interactive computer performer designed to follow a live performer, adapt to timing changes, and create a sense of ensemble interaction. The system is primarily a follower rather than an improviser, but this limitation is useful for IT4s because it clarifies the difference between accompaniment/following systems and systems that attempt turn-based response generation. The paper supports the importance of timing, responsiveness, and tolerance of performer variation.
