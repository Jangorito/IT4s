## Summary

Pasquier et al. introduce musical metacreation as a field concerned with computational systems that perform creative musical tasks such as composition, improvisation, accompaniment, and interpretation. The paper surveys the breadth of the field, from autonomous generative systems to interactive systems. It is a foundational source for framing IT4s as part of musical computational creativity while being precise that IT4s is an interactive, turn-based prototype rather than a fully autonomous creative agent.
